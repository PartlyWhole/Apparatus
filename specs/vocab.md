# Vocabulary and What It Measures  (`vocab`, `measure`)

Status: live · Code: `exercises/vocab.js`, `exercises/measure.js` (+ `lib/forms.js`)

## Vocabulary (`vocab`)
The chapter's 13 terms (base unit, derived unit, International System of Units, kilogram, liter, meter, metric prefix, metric system, right circular cylinder, right rectangular solid, second, SI System, unit conversion factor). Each term is its own idea, asked two ways, all multiple choice:
- **Clue → term:** a short description that doesn't give the word away; four terms, the close one included (base ↔ derived, cylinder ↔ rectangular solid). Never two right answers: the three names for the SI (SI System, International System of Units, metric system) don't appear for each other, and "base unit" isn't offered for kilogram, meter or second.
- **A creative question per term:** a drawn cylinder or box ("This shape is a…"), the volume formula (V = πr²h, V = L · W · H), a conversion fraction drawn out (100 cm / 1 m), odd one out ("Which one is a base unit?"), traps ("A kilogram measures… mass / weight"), and the history (France; 1960; Sèvres).
Every term gated (26 questions with no misses); the next-session check asks every clue again.

## What It Measures (`measure`)
Unit → quantity ("cm³ measures… length / area / volume / mass") and quantity → unit ("Which one measures volume? cm / cm³ / N / day"). Ideas: length, volume, mass, time, the other base units (K, A, mol, cd), derived (N, J). Traps: weight for mass units (feedback: weight is a force, in newtons), area/length for volume, look-alike units (cm vs cm³, m vs m³, mm vs mL). 12 questions with no misses.

## Cheat sheets
Vocabulary: every term with its clue, A to Z. What It Measures: each quantity with its units.
